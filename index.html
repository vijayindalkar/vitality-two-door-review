Two-Door landing pages: design review

The nine landing and follow-up pages for Vitality Orthopedics, in the design they have now. The surveys, the booking calendar, the guide form and the page buttons are pictures here, so nothing can be submitted or booked. This copy is not indexed by search engines.

The survey card says how many steps there are and shows a progress bar (the survey pictures are four questions in, so the bar is part-filled). The hero now shows a small round portrait of Dr. Ziegler with his titles beside it, instead of a large photo.
