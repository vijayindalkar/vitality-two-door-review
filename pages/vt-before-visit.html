Your visit, step by step

Plan on about an hour, sometimes longer. You don’t have to decide anything before you leave.

  1. 01

    Check in

    Arrive about 15 minutes early. The front desk gets you settled.

  2. 02

    The exam

    Dr. Ziegler examines your knee or shoulder, looks at it under ultrasound when it helps and goes over any MRI or x-rays with you.

  3. 03

    What’s causing it

    He tells you what he thinks is going on, in plain language, and what’s likely if you leave it alone.

  4. 04

    Your options

    The treatments that fit your case, with and without surgery, and the pros and cons of each. If surgery is the better answer, he’ll say so and explain why.

  5. 05

    Starting that day, if it fits

    Some non-surgical treatments can start before you leave, if you and Dr. Ziegler decide they make sense.

  6. 06

    Your plan and price

    If you want to move forward, you get a flat price up front, and the office helps you with scheduling.

Your treatment options

Dr. Ziegler treats knees and shoulders with surgery and without it. You don’t need to pick before you come in. That’s what the visit is for.

If non-surgical treatment fits

Most of what you’ve tried so far, like cortisone and pain relievers, is aimed at the pain. Regenerative treatment has a different goal: material from your own body, placed under ultrasound into the damaged area, to support your body’s own repair.

If surgery is the right call

The Vitality Orthopedic Recovery System

For surgical patients, Dr. Ziegler puts everything into one plan he calls the Vitality Orthopedic Recovery System. You don’t need to remember the name. It means the weeks before your surgery, the operation and your recovery are planned together, by the surgeon who does the operation.

Before surgery

Strength and motion work and, when they make sense for you, hyperbaric oxygen, biologic treatment, and nutrition and recovery protocols, so your body is as ready as it can be.

The operation

Minimally invasive techniques to repair what’s damaged in your knee or shoulder, done by Dr. Ziegler himself.

After surgery

A structured follow-up plan with rehab, biologic support and, when appropriate, hyperbaric oxygen and other recovery therapies, with Dr. Ziegler at every follow-up.

You get a flat price up front for your surgery and your recovery plan, together or separately, before you commit.

What to bring

Think about

  • What you want to get back to, and by when.
  • How long it’s been going on, and what made it worse.
  • Every treatment you’ve tried, and how it went.

Bring with you

  • MRI or x-ray reports, and a list of your medications.
  • Your questions, written down.
  • Your spouse or partner, if they help you decide.
  • Whatever you’d need to get started that day, if you think you might.

Wear loose, comfortable clothes so Dr. Ziegler can examine the joint, and arrive about 15 minutes early.

Cost and other questions

Does insurance cover any of this?

No. Dr. Ziegler’s care is self-pay, surgery included, and he doesn’t bill insurance.

How much will treatment cost?

It depends on your plan. You get a flat price up front, before you commit to anything, and the office goes over it with you.

Does the treatment hurt?

You’ll feel pressure during an injection, and most people are sore for a few days after. Dr. Ziegler goes over what to expect for your treatment at the visit.

How much time will I miss, and when will I notice a change?

It depends on the treatment. Dr. Ziegler will tell you what’s realistic for your knee or shoulder once he’s examined you.

What if I’m not a candidate?

Dr. Ziegler will tell you why, and what he’d do instead.

Will I see Dr. Ziegler himself?

Yes, at this visit and at your follow-ups.
