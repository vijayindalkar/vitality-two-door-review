How to get ready

Think about

  • When it started, and what made it worse.
  • What you miss doing most.
  • Your questions, cost included.

Have with you

  • Your calendar, so you can book the evaluation if it’s a fit.
  • Where any MRI or x-ray was done, so the office can request the report.
  • Your spouse or partner on speaker, if they help you decide.

Be somewhere quiet, and not driving.

Cost and other questions

Is this a sales call?

No. Nobody will ask you to start treatment on the phone. Dr. Ziegler listens, explains how his care works and books your evaluation if it makes sense.

Does insurance cover any of it?

No. Dr. Ziegler’s care is self-pay, surgery included, and he doesn’t bill insurance.

What will it cost?

The call is free. Dr. Ziegler walks you through the investment for the evaluation on the call, and at the evaluation you get a flat price for your plan before you commit to anything.

What if I’m not a candidate?

He’ll tell you, and why. Fifteen minutes is a fair trade for knowing where you stand.

Will he have my answers?

Yes. What you told us will be in front of him, so you won’t have to start from scratch.
