Questions people ask before they book

Is this treatment safe?

Platelet-rich plasma and bone marrow concentrate come from your own body, so there’s no donor material for your body to reject. The most common side effect is soreness for a few days where the injection went in. Dr. Ziegler goes over the risks for your situation at your evaluation.

What does it cost, and does insurance cover any of it?

The call is free. His team walks you through the investment for the evaluation and any treatment on the call. At the evaluation, you get a flat price for your plan before you commit to anything.

Does it hurt, and how much time will I miss?

You’ll feel pressure during the injection. Treatment usually takes one to two hours in the office, and most people go home the same day. Dr. Ziegler gives you a plan for the weeks after.

How long do results last?

It depends on the joint and how much damage there is, so Dr. Ziegler won’t promise a number. He’ll tell you what’s realistic once he’s examined you.

I was told my knee is bone on bone. Is a call still worth it?

Yes. Plenty of people hear “bone on bone” and still have options. Dr. Ziegler will examine the knee and tell you whether non-surgical treatment is worth trying, or whether it’s time to plan for something else.

Do I need an MRI or x-ray first?

No. If you have one, bring the report to your evaluation. If you need new imaging, Dr. Ziegler will tell you.

What if I’m not a candidate?

Dr. Ziegler will tell you at your evaluation and explain why. He’s a surgeon, so if an operation is the better answer, he’ll tell you that too.

Is this stem cell therapy?

Bone marrow concentrate is what most people mean by stem cell therapy. Dr. Ziegler draws it from the back of your hip and places it in the same visit. Nothing comes from an outside supplier.
